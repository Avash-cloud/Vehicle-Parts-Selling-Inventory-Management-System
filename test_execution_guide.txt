Test Execution Guide for Documentation

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on where to go in the system, what actions to perform, and what data to insert for each of your 16 test cases. You can use these steps directly in your documentation.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
Test Case 1: Financial Report Management (FT-01)
- Role: Admin
- Where to go: Navigate to the Admin Dashboard -> "Reports" or "Financial Reports" section.
- What to do: Select a date range (Daily, Monthly, Yearly) from the filter options and click "Generate Report".
- What to insert: 
  - Start Date: 01-Jan-2026
  - End Date: 31-Jan-2026
  - Report Type: Monthly

-------------------------------------------------------------------

- What to do: Fill out the staff details, assign a role, and save.
- What to insert:

  - Phone: 9876543210

Test Case 10: Loyalty Program System (FT-10)
- Role: Staff (during billing)
- Where to go: Staff Dashboard -> "Sales" -> "Create Sales Invoice"
- What to do: Create a bill with a total amount exceeding 5000 and check if a 10% discount is applied automatically at checkout.
- What to insert:
  - Add parts totaling e.g., 6000 (like 4 Engine Oils at 1500 each).
  - Observe subtotal: 6000, Discount: 600, Final Total: 5400.

Test Case 11: Customer History Management (FT-11)
- Role: Staff
- Where to go: Staff Dashboard -> "Customers" -> Click on a specific customer's profile (e.g., Alice Johnson).
- What to do: Navigate to the "History" or "Transactions" tab inside the customer profile to view past sales and vehicles.
- What to insert: None (just click and view).

Test Case 15: Low Stock and Credit Reminder Notification (FT-15)
- Role: Admin/System
- Where to go: Admin Dashboard -> "Notifications" or Email Inbox.
- What to do: For low stock, sell an item until its stock goes below the threshold (e.g., 5). Verify an alert pops up or an email is received. For credit, check the email of a customer who has pending dues.
- What to insert:
  - Sell 48 Brake Pads (if stock is 50, threshold is 5) so remaining is 2. Wait for alert.

Test Case 16: Purchase and Service History (FT-16)
- Role: Customer
- Where to go: Customer Portal -> "My History" or "My Orders"
- What to do: Log in as a customer and view the list of past purchases and service records.
- What to insert: None (just browse the history tables).
